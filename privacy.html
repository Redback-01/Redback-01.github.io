1. WHO WE ARE

[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS], United Kingdom, is responsible for the personal data described here ("we", "us"). Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]. [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER, if you register with the ICO.]

2. THE SHORT VERSION

Weflin runs on your PC. What you type, say and make (chats, prompts, pictures, speech, microphone audio, video, CapCut drafts) stays on your PC. We do not collect it, and Weflin has no analytics or tracking. The only information that leaves your PC because of Weflin is described in section 3.

3. WHAT GOES OVER THE INTERNET

3.1 Buying Weflin. You buy Weflin from [LEMON SQUEEZY, OR YOUR CHOSEN PROVIDER], which sells it to you as merchant of record and collects your payment details, name, email, billing address and tax details. They are a separate controller of that data under their own privacy policy ([LINK]). We receive from them what we need to run the licence and support you: your name, email, country, order details and licence key.

3.2 Activating and checking your licence. When you activate Weflin, and about once a day after that, Weflin sends to the licence service: your licence key, and a name for your PC made from your computer's name plus a short code (so you can recognise your PCs and we can enforce the number of PCs a key allows). Their servers also see your IP address as for any internet connection. The licence service is run by [PROVIDER], which processes this for us and may do so outside the UK (for example in the United States) under their own safeguards.

3.3 Downloads. When you install components, your PC downloads them straight from their publishers: GitHub (Strata and ComfyUI programs), Hugging Face (AI models and the speech and voice-input models the first time you use them), and PyTorch's download server. These companies can see your IP address and what you download, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive any of that.

3.4 Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you tell us. Please do not send files containing other people's personal data unless necessary.

3.5 Weflin does not check for updates in the background and sends no crash reports.

4. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PC

Weflin saves on your PC: your settings; your saved chats; your batches, pictures, speech and video files in the folders you can see in Weflin; and a licence file in %APPDATA%\StrataDesktop that records your key, your name or email as the licence service gave it, and dates. These are yours; you can delete them at any time. A fingerprint made from your PC's identifier is used on your PC only, to protect the licence file; it is not sent to us. Voice input: your microphone is used only while voice mode or the microphone button is on; audio is turned into text on your PC and is not sent anywhere.

5. WHY WE USE YOUR DATA AND OUR LEGAL BASIS (UK GDPR)

  • To provide Weflin and your licence, and to give support: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep records for tax and accounting: legal obligation.
  • To prevent key sharing and fraud (activation limits, refund and chargeback handling): our legitimate interest in protecting the business.

6. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Order and licence records: [6 years] after your purchase (UK tax and accounting rules; confirm with your accountant). Support emails: [2 years] after the last message. The licence service keeps its own records under its own policy.

7. WHO ELSE SEES IT

Only the providers named above, and where the law requires it (for example tax authorities or a court). We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it (we may need to keep records the law requires), to restrict or object to our use of it, and to move it. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your own country's data protection authority.

9. CHILDREN

Weflin is not for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. CHANGES

If we change this notice we will update the version and date above; for changes that matter we will tell you in Weflin or by email.
