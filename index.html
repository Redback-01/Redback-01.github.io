WHAT IT DOESEverything for a content workflow

Built for people who make videos and need a lot of material: a script goes in, and pictures, voice-over, clips and an editable timeline come out.

Chat

A capable language model that runs locally, for scripts, ideas and edits. Talk to it with your microphone and have it talk back.

Images

Describe a picture and get it in seconds, using Qwen-Image. Choose the shape and quality, or let the chat model write a better prompt for you.

Speech

Natural narration in a choice of voices, using Kokoro. Paste a script, pick a voice and speed, and save the audio.

Video clips

Turn any picture into a short animated clip with Wan 2.2, at 480p or 720p, in portrait or landscape.

Batches

Queue a whole script at once: one picture, one narration and one clip per scene, numbered and saved in order. Pause, resume or retry, and carry on after a restart.

CapCut timeline

Weflin builds a CapCut project with each scene's clip and narration lined up on the timeline, ready for you to finish in CapCut.

IN USEPictures, voices, timelines

The same dark, simple layout throughout. Each tool has a single-item screen and a batch screen.

HOW IT WORKSFrom purchase to first picture

Buy a licenceYou get a licence key by email. One key works on the number of PCs shown at checkout, and you can move it between PCs.
Install WeflinA normal Windows installer. It is small because the AI models are not inside it.
Press Install in SetupWeflin checks your PC, then downloads each piece straight from its publisher and verifies it. It can be stopped and resumed. This is the long step: allow a few hours on a typical connection.
Start creatingEverything runs on your PC from then on. No account to log in to, and no per-image or per-minute fees.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PCNothing leaves your computer

Your prompts, chats, pictures, voice recordings and video are processed on your own computer and are not sent to us. Weflin has no analytics. The only things that go over the internet are the licence check and the downloads from the AI models' publishers. You own what you make; see the licence agreement and privacy notice.

The AI models are made by other people (for example Qwen, Wan, Kokoro and Whisper) and keep their own licences, which Weflin lists in the app under Settings > Legal.

"Words per second" is how fast the chat model writes (measured in tokens, about 4 for every 3 words), far faster than anyone reads. A 10-scene video

Ten pictures and ten 4-second clips take about 44 minutes on an RTX 3060, about 21 minutes on an RTX 4070, and about 13 minutes on an RTX 5070 Ti, plus the narration. Leave it running overnight, or queue it and do something else.

Why 12 GB works

The tested 12 GB cards used about 11.5 GB at their peak and still finished every job, with no failures. Close other programs that use the graphics card (games, browsers with lots of tabs) before a long video batch.

Not yet tested

Graphics cards with 8 GB or less, PCs with 32 GB of memory, AMD and Intel graphics, and laptops. If you try one, tell us how it went.

QUESTIONSGood to know

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

Weflin is a new app from a small independent developer, and Windows warns about any installer it has not seen many people download. It is not a sign that something is wrong. To go ahead, click More info and then Run anyway. If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded", choose Keep. If you would rather not, ask for a refund within the period in the refund policy. We plan to sign the installer once Weflin has an established customer base.

Does it need a subscription?

No. You pay once for a licence. The models Weflin uses run on your PC, so there are no per-image or per-minute charges.

Are the AI models included?

No, and that keeps the installer small. Weflin downloads them from their original publishers when you press Install, checks each file, and can resume if your connection drops. They have their own licences, which you can read in the app.

Can I use what I make commercially?

You own what you make with Weflin, and you can publish or sell it, but you are responsible for it. The AI models have their own licences with their own terms, so please read them. Details are in the licence agreement.

What am I not allowed to make with it?

Weflin is a general creative tool that runs on your own PC, and you are responsible for what you make. You must not use it to break the law, to infringe other people's rights, to make sexual content involving children, to harass or defraud anyone, or to present generated people, voices or events as real in order to mislead or harm. This is set out in section 2 of the licence agreement. Weflin does not clone voices: it speaks with a fixed set of built-in voices.

How many PCs can I use it on?

The number shown at checkout. To move your key to another PC, deactivate it in the app first (Settings > Licence).

What if it does not work on my PC?

Ask for a refund within the period in the refund policy, with no questions. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for everything except installing and the licence check. After a successful check Weflin keeps working offline for up to 14 days.

Does it work with CapCut?

Weflin creates a CapCut project file with your clips and narration on the timeline. CapCut is a separate product made by someone else; Weflin is not connected with it. Close CapCut before you create a draft.
